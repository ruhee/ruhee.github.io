<p style="text-align: center;">* * *</p>
 
At [Zine Dump](https://zinedump.fyi/) last week, I bought a great zine by Oren Wry called [NBA PLAYER TATTOO ENCYCLOPEDIA]({{ site.url }}/assets/posts/2026-11-14-zine.jpg); it was being sold at the community table, so the sellers weren't the zine makers. I made conversation with one of the sellers about how I was into this zine because I also did silly esoteric sports research, and mentioned something vague about mustaches and baseball players and their on-field performance.

The person at the table was like, "oh, mustaches huh? You know, there's that guy on the Jays who had that huge &rsquo;stache at the beginning of the season, and then he got rid of it and he had a beard after, you should study him!" And at the same time we said "Dylan Cease!"

And then I showed them the [website](https://stachecast.lol). (Could not have scripted that any better.)

I'm having so much fun building that stupid website and showing it to people. For version 2, I built a little script that consumes my CSV and dumps out a graph-friendly file, but then I realized my math was _super_ wrong, and ... like, it really doesn't matter, but it's funnier to me if the data actually holds up. So now I have a script that takes a CSV of, uh, every pitch Cease has thrown in the majors (about 21,000 of them, fyi) and does the math on that instead. Things got out of hand.

I'm just enjoying having a little project to poke away at, especially one that makes me laugh pretty much every time I work on it. I've learned about calculating weighted averages, node's sqlite package, streaming CSVs, CSS `clamp()` and `background-clip: text;` and so many other things already. New version with all 8 seasons is probably coming right around the lockout, so stay tuned.

Also, now that I've done all this plumbing to calculate the values on the fly, v3 could absolutely expand to other players. Do any other current MLB players come to mind who regularly appear with at least two different facial hair styles? (Doesn't need to be a pitcher!) If so... I would like to know.

<p style="text-align: center;">* * *</p>

I tend to listen to songs on repeat a _lot_ if I really like them. My current list of repeats hasn't changed too much lately: 

- ["She's Leaving You" by MJ Lenderman](https://mjlenderman.bandcamp.com/track/shes-leaving-you) from _Manning Fireworks_
- ["Evil Spawn" by Waxahatchee](https://waxahatchee.bandcamp.com/track/evil-spawn) from _Tigers Blood_
- ["Big, Dumb" by Restorations](http://restorations.bandcamp.com/track/big-dumb) from _Restorations_
- ["Almost There" by Brickie](https://brickie.bandcamp.com/track/almost-there) from _BRICKIE_

![A last.fm screenshot showing the above mentioned songs as my top 4 in the last 180 days]({{ site.url }}/assets/posts/2026-11-14-lastfm.png "A last.fm screenshot showing the above mentioned songs as my top 4 in the last 180 days")

I love the chorus to "She's Leaving You" (I am the 75,000th person to think this, I know) but I also, particularly, love the bar right before the chorus—I instinctively want the break to hit on 3, but it waits until 4, and I find that so satisfying.

I also love when songs just ride the I chord, sue me, and "Big, Dumb" comes in with such a great intro (at about 1:05) where they are just hammering the root and I can feel it blowing my hair back from my face. Same thing at 3:25. Great stuff, make sure it's turned up loud.

The repeating is a quirk of my brain, I think, but I also enjoy doing it because after that many listens I pay attention to things that I didn't necessarily notice at first. Consumption is so passive these days, and I have access to what seems like the majority of recorded music on my phone, so why sit with the same thing for so long? But I enjoy the feeling of climbing inside a song I love and noticing the little details, especially the not-altogether-intentional ones (one of my favourites is the intake of breath you can hear before the spelling part in [the Constantines' "Hyacinth Blues"](https://open.spotify.com/track/00kB5rhDREfdwBBDmhWaio)). If a song comes to mind for you as you're reading this, I recommend looping it and seeing else what you notice!

[please](mailto:ruhee.dewji@gmail.com).
- I've been trying to consume less American politics and news, which feels almost impossible on the internet, especially if you use your social media to keep up with US-based sports. (I have a million muted terms on Bluesky including "Democrats", "Republicans", "White House", and the names of like 20 individual politicians.) I'm also trying to fill that space with Canadian news instead. Lately I've been catching up on [The Hatchet](https://hatchetmedia.ca/)'s podcast—they have a very good [series on Canadian policing](https://hatchetmedia.ca/t/a-few-bad-apples) (bleak as hell, as you can imagine). [The episode on the police killing of Montreal teen Nooran Rezayi](https://hatchetmedia.ca/p/the-killing-of-nooran-rezayi-a-few) was both extremely good and _extremely_ depressing; Arshy Mann rightly calls out the lack of national coverage on stories like this, where they remain "local news" because the Longueuil police shooting a kid in broad daylight is considered only relevant to Longueuil, I guess. If you learn just one or two facts about policing anywhere in this country and it doesn't radicalize you, I can't believe it.
